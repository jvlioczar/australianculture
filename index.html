first, the land

A country the size of a continent

This is Australia.

7.69 million km², the 6th biggest country in the world. It’s also the largest island and the smallest continent on the planet.

Let’s compare it with Brazil, the fifth-largest country.

Almost the same size: 8.5 million km². The difference is who lives there.

213 million versus 28 million.

For every Australian there are almost eight Brazilians. A huge land with very few people, and an interior that’s nearly empty: the outback.

Nearly everyone lives by the sea.

Around 85% of people live within 50 km of the coast. Each dot is one of the 25 biggest cities. Notice the empty middle.

Sources: ABS, population (Mar 2026), ABS, regional population (2023), IBGE, population of Brazil and Natural Earth (map).

    and the people

    Who are the Australians?

    Out of every 100 people, 32 were born overseas.

    Post-war migration turned a mostly British country into one of the most multicultural in the world. Today people from almost 200 nationalities live here, and more than 300 languages are spoken at home.

    3.2%identify as Aboriginal and/or Torres Strait Islander
    36.7%have both parents born overseas
