about the guide

G'day, I'm Julio.

This guide was born from wanting to explain Australia to anyone curious to know the country and its people a little better.

Australian Culture is a special project of BiblioVerse, created by Julio Cesar Prava.

Julio is a graphic designer and content creator, a Brazilian living in Brisbane. On @gdayjulio he shares tips on studying, working and living in Australia, along with light-hearted videos about the country.
